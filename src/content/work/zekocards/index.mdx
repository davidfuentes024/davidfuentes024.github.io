A Minigames platform for learning Japanese, designed to require at least a little bit of effort—using constant repetition and a certain level of challenge to actually learn something.

I’ve tried various other platforms, but after using them for a while, I realized that I had learned absolutely nothing. On top of that, some of them even required subscriptions just to get "energy" so you could keep "learning."

Honestly, that was pretty frustrating. I just wanted to learn some hiragana and a few everyday words, so I decided to create a solution that would simply let me do some repetitive exercises without having to sit through an ad every three words... and, of course, include my own pet.

0 ads, 0 tracking, 100 free
